HOW TO PLAY

On a computer

Play with the keyboard: each game's card shows its keys. P or Esc pauses.

On a phone

Turn your phone sideways and play with your thumbs. The touch controls are on each card too.

High scores

Kept in this browser on this device, with your initials. Your best shows on each card. Settings such as volume carry over to every game.

Full screen

In a game, tap the full-screen button to hide the browser's bars. Or add the arcade to your phone's home screen and it opens like an app: on an iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, open the browser's menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
