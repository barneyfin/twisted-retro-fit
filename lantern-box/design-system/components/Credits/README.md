# Credits

Who made the game, its music, sound and type, from Settings. Version 12, step 5b round 3 (Barney, 7 November 2026); the studio credit is ARCADE.md's one exception to no image files (decided 7 November 2026).

## Markup
A dialog plaque (520 wide; 720 in two columns on a phone held sideways): the heading and rule, the studio's strip (`lb-credit-strip`, the Brand asset `lbg-credit.svg`, 300px wide, 240 on a phone, alt "Made by Lantern Box Games"), then `lb-credits` of `lb-credit` (a `k` label, the `v` line, an `s` note), then Back.

## Rules
- **Music**: "Music made with Google Gemini", with both tracks, The Long Vigil and Lantern in the Moor. Never Barney as composer: Google's terms forbid presenting AI music as made by a person (PRD question 10).
- **Sound**: "Two sound effects from Pixabay; the rest made in code" (the hurt and the crows' caws, Sound).
- **Type**: IM Fell English, by Igino Marini · Alegreya Sans, by Huerta Tipográfica, under the SIL Open Font Licence (Licences).
- **The studio**: every arcade game carries the same outlined credit from the repo's brand/ folder, built in as a soundtrack is, never redrawn (ARCADE.md). This system holds a copy in the Brand group for the previews.
- **Keys** (computers): Backspace or Enter goes back to Settings. Never Esc.
