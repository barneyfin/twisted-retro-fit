# HowToPlay

The how-to-play page: the goal in two lines, then the controls; from the "?" tile in play (the game paused) or the title's How to play. Agreed in step 4b round 11.

## Markup
A dialog plaque (560 wide; on a phone 700 wide in two columns) headed How to play, the rule, the goal (`lb-howto-goal`), the keys (`lb-keys` of `lb-key`) or on a phone the touch controls drawn small (`lb-touchkeys` of `lb-touchkey`), and the button.

## Rules
- The goal, Barney's words: "Cut your path through four blighted lands, felling the terror bound to each. Sever the roots of The Gaping Tree to break the night." over "Three chances to break the dark. Fall, and return to the glow of your last lit lantern."
- Keys: A D or the arrows, walk; W or up, climb a bank; Space, jump; Shift, roll; J, attack; K, cast; L, switch spell; Esc or P, pause. A phone shows the stick and buttons instead, and a computer no touch controls.
- From play the button is Resume; from the title, Back. Esc does the same.
