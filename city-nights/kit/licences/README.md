The SIL Open Font License texts for the five fonts in `fonts/`. Every game built with City Nights carries these alongside its embedded fonts.

- Antonio and IBM Plex Sans Condensed are the whole fonts, converted to WOFF2 with no glyphs removed. Plex's licence reserves the name "Plex", so it is never cut down here.
- Noto Sans TC Bold and Noto Serif TC Black are fixed at one weight and cut down to the characters on the word list. Neither name uses a reserved font name, so the cut-down fonts keep their names.
- Bangers is Fontsource's Latin cut of the font (@fontsource/bangers 5.3.0, 222 characters), used as published. Its licence reserves no font name. It is for the crash pop's letters only.
